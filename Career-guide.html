Prepare For Your First Remote Job Search

A calm, practical checklist for beginners — no laptop needed

By Nexora Team • Nov 25, 2026 • 5 min read

Everyone wants a remote job, but most people fail because they apply without preparing. Don't be that person. If you follow this checklist, you will be ahead of 90% of applicants.

1. Fix Your CV First (Before You Apply)

Your CV is not your life story. For remote jobs, keep it to 1 page:

✅ Your name, WhatsApp, Email, Location (e.g. Athi River, Kenya)
✅ 3 skills you can actually do (e.g. Data Entry, Social Media, Writing)
✅ 1-2 examples of work you have done — even free work counts
❌ Don't lie. Recruiters check.

2. Create Your 3 Free Accounts

You only need these 3 to start:

1. Upwork - For freelance jobs
2. Fiverr - To sell a small service
3. LinkedIn - To look professional and be found

Use the SAME photo and SAME name on all 3. It builds trust.

🔥 Nexora Tip: Don't wait for a perfect CV. A simple, honest CV today is better than a perfect CV next month.

3. Learn One Skill Well

Don't try to learn 10 things. Pick ONE for 30 days:

• Data Entry / Virtual Assistant
• Social Media Management
• Content Writing
• Basic Video Editing

Use freeCodeCamp, Google Skillshop, and YouTube to learn free.

4. How To Apply (The Right Way)

Don't copy-paste the same message to 100 jobs. For each job, write 3 lines:

Line 1: I can do this job because...
Line 2: I have done similar work here...
Line 3: I can start today, here is my sample...

5. Avoid Scams - Read This!

Real remote jobs will NEVER ask you to pay money to get the job. If they ask for "activation fee", "training fee" or "M-PESA deposit", it's a SCAM. Walk away.

Your 7-Day Checklist

☐ Day 1: Fix CV
☐ Day 2: Create Upwork + Fiverr + LinkedIn
☐ Day 3: Pick 1 skill, start learning
☐ Day 4: Make 1 sample of your work
☐ Day 5: Apply to 5 jobs
☐ Day 6: Apply to 5 more jobs
☐ Day 7: Follow up + keep learning

If you do this for 30 days, you will get replies. It takes patience, not luck.
